# YUTEMU Brand Guideline

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Working Brand Foundation
**Product:** Playable Wedding Experience

---

## 1. Brand Essence

### Brand name
**YUTEMU**

### Core idea
YUTEMU turns a wedding invitation into an experience people can **enter, explore, and remember**.

It combines:

- wedding invitation
- interactive storytelling
- a social 2D world
- lightweight game mechanics
- wedding information and RSVP

YUTEMU should feel like **a tiny world made for one couple and the people they love**.

### Brand promise
**Don't just invite people to the wedding. Let them enter the story.**

### Primary tagline
**Temui kisah mereka.**

### Secondary tagline
**Your love story, playable.**

Use the Indonesian tagline for consumer-facing Indonesia-first communication. Use the English tagline selectively for product, technology, partnership, or global-facing contexts.

---

# 2. Positioning

## Category
**Playable Wedding Experience**

Avoid positioning YUTEMU primarily as:

- digital invitation platform
- wedding website builder
- online RSVP tool
- mini-game invitation

Those descriptions understate the product.

## Positioning statement
> **YUTEMU is a playable wedding experience that lets guests step inside a couple's story, explore their world, interact with it, and still access every important wedding detail in one place.**

## Category contrast

| Traditional wedding invitation | YUTEMU |
|---|---|
| Read | Enter |
| Scroll | Explore |
| Static story | Playable story |
| Information page | Living world |
| RSVP form | Social experience |
| Guest watches | Guest participates |
| Template | Configurable world |

---

# 3. Brand Personality

YUTEMU is:

### Playful
It invites curiosity without becoming childish.

### Warm
It feels personal, intimate, and human.

### Magical
There should be a small sense of wonder when the world opens.

### Modern
It behaves like a polished digital product, not an old-style wedding template.

### Elegant
The product can be playful without becoming visually noisy or tacky.

### Personal
The couple and their story remain the emotional center.

---

# 4. Personality Boundaries

YUTEMU is **not**:

- childish
- overly cute
- generic “AI-generated” fantasy
- casino-like or reward-heavy
- hyper-gamified
- wedding-template corporate
- visually overloaded
- dependent on jokes or memes

### Desired balance

**Playful × Elegant**

**Game × Romance**

**Personal × Scalable**

**Magical × Believable**

# 7. Naming System

Use YUTEMU as the master brand.

### Recommended product modules

**Yutemu World**
The interactive 2D wedding world.

**Yutemu Book**
The formal wedding invitation and information layer.

**Yutemu Story**
The couple's narrative and milestone experience.

**Yutemu RSVP**
Attendance confirmation.

**Yutemu Guestbook**
Messages from guests.

**Yutemu Studio**
Admin/configuration product for creating a wedding experience.

### Naming rule
Use **Yutemu + simple functional noun**.

Avoid excessive sub-brands such as:

- YutemuX
- Yutemu Pro Max
- Yutemu Hyper World
- Yutemu Galaxy

The product should feel like one coherent platform.

---

# 8. Logo Direction

## Wordmark
Primary logo should be a clean custom wordmark:

**YUTEMU**

All-caps is recommended for the master wordmark because it gives the brand a crisp, modern, game-studio/product feel.

## Logo personality

- rounded but not bubbly
- geometric but not sterile
- distinctive `Y` and/or `U` construction
- readable at small sizes
- works in monochrome
- should feel equally credible in a wedding invitation and a game UI

## Avoid

- wedding rings as the main logo icon
- hearts as the primary logo device
- roses
- silhouettes of bride and groom
- excessive Japanese motifs
- literal game-controller icons
- random pixel-art typography

The product may use small game/world motifs in the visual system, but the **master logo should remain timeless**.

---

# 9. Symbol / Icon Direction

The brand can use a secondary symbol derived from the idea of:

**two paths meeting**

or

**two points becoming one world**.

Possible visual concept:

- two rounded paths converging
- two small points entering a shared shape
- a doorway/portal suggesting “enter the story”
- an abstract `Y` formed by two paths

The symbol should work without the wordmark as an app icon or favicon.

# 10. Color Strategy

YUTEMU should not depend on one fixed wedding palette. Each wedding can have its own theme.

Therefore the brand has a **core interface palette** and a **wedding theme palette**.

## Core brand palette

### Ink
**#171719**

Use for primary text, logo, deep UI surfaces.

### Paper
**#FFFDF8**

Warm near-white. Prefer over pure white for editorial/invitation surfaces.

### Mist
**#F1F0EC**

Neutral supporting surface.

### Accent Violet
**#6D5CE7**

Primary product accent for YUTEMU-owned UI elements.

### Accent Rose
**#E8899D**

Secondary romantic accent.

### Success
**#3E9B73**

Use sparingly and semantically.

### Warning
**#D39B38**

Use semantically, not decoratively.

## Color rule
The core YUTEMU product can use the brand accent, while an individual wedding may override the visual theme for its world and invitation.

The YUTEMU logo itself should normally remain neutral unless used in a controlled marketing composition.

---

# 11. Typography

## Primary type personality
Use a modern humanist/geometric sans-serif for product UI.

Desired traits:

- excellent mobile legibility
- rounded but mature
- strong numerals
- good Indonesian glyph coverage
- works at small sizes

## Editorial / romance layer
A restrained serif may be used for:

- couple names
- chapter titles
- story moments
- wedding editorial sections

Do not use a decorative script font as the default product typography.

## Hierarchy

**Display:** Couple names / major emotional moments

**Heading:** Location, chapter, section

**Body:** Information and story

**UI:** Navigation, buttons, labels, status

---

# 12. Imagery

## Photography
Photography should feel:

- real
- intimate
- candid where possible
- warm
- cinematic without looking like stock photography

The couple's real photographs outrank generic stock imagery.

## World art
The 2D world can use stylized art, but it should feel like a coherent authored game world rather than a collection of unrelated assets.

### Desired
- soft environmental storytelling
- readable silhouettes
- recognizable landmarks
- small visual details that reward exploration
- subtle ambient motion

### Avoid
- asset-pack collage appearance
- excessive particles
- neon everywhere
- generic fantasy castles
- random RPG loot aesthetics
- visual clutter that hides navigation

---

# 13. Brand + Game Relationship

YUTEMU is **game-inspired**, not a generic RPG brand.

The game layer exists to make the wedding story memorable.

### Therefore

**Story > Game mechanics**

**Emotion > Competition**

**Exploration > Grinding**

**Social presence > Chat spam**

**Discovery > Difficulty**

### No identity drift
The wedding should never feel like the guest accidentally entered a generic fantasy MMORPG.

Every game mechanic should reinforce:

- the couple
- their story
- the venue
- the celebration
- the relationship between guests

# 18. Motion Principles

Motion should communicate:

- arrival
- discovery
- warmth
- life

Use:

- subtle environmental movement
- smooth modal transitions
- small interaction feedback
- soft camera movement
- restrained particle effects

Avoid:

- constant bouncing
- excessive screen shake
- reward explosions for mundane actions
- forced animations on every tap

Reduced-motion settings must significantly reduce ambient and decorative motion.

---

# 19. Brand in the Game World

The YUTEMU brand should remain mostly invisible inside a couple's world.

The world belongs to the couple.

Use YUTEMU branding for:

- loading
- system UI
- onboarding
- fallback invitation
- branded share cards
- studio/admin

Do not plaster `YUTEMU` onto buildings, NPCs, signs, or environments unless it is narratively justified.

---

# 20. Wedding Theme Customization

Every published wedding can customize:

- couple names
- photographs
- story
- world decoration theme
- NPC appearance
- dialogue
- colors
- typography choices within safe bounds
- music/audio
- wedding information
- gallery
- RSVP settings

But the product remains recognizably YUTEMU through:

- interaction quality
- UX patterns
- navigation structure
- accessibility
- technical consistency
- system components

---

# 21. Premium Design Principles

YUTEMU should feel premium through **restraint**, not complexity.

### Premium means

- fewer but better effects
- consistent spacing
- high-quality typography
- clear hierarchy
- purposeful animation
- excellent photography
- believable world design
- fast loading
- polished transitions

### Premium does not mean

- gold everywhere
- cursive everywhere
- gradients everywhere
- glassmorphism everywhere
- giant headings everywhere
- particle explosions

---

# 22. Accessibility

Brand quality includes accessibility.

Required principles:

- sufficient text contrast
- readable mobile type sizes
- accessible Wedding Book path
- keyboard support on desktop
- reduced-motion support
- touch targets large enough for mobile
- no critical information locked behind gameplay
- clear fallback when the game cannot load

YUTEMU should never force a guest to play before they can find:

- event details
- date/time
- location
- RSVP
- essential wedding information

# 24. Brand Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Make people curious | Explain everything immediately |
| Keep the couple central | Make the platform the star |
| Use playful interactions | Add random game mechanics |
| Use elegant UI | Make it look like a generic game HUD |
| Let guests explore | Force exploration for critical information |
| Use real couple photography | Fill everything with stock photos |
| Keep motion subtle | Animate everything |
| Use clear copy | Use marketing jargon |
| Make each world personal | Reuse identical visual identity everywhere |
| Preserve YUTEMU system quality | Fork the product for every couple |

---

# 25. Brand North Star

Every significant design decision should pass this question:

> **Does this make the guest feel closer to the couple's story?**

If yes, it belongs.

If it only exists because it is a “cool game feature,” reconsider it.

---

# 26. Final Brand Definition

**YUTEMU is the place where a wedding invitation becomes a world.**

People do not simply open YUTEMU.

They **enter** it.

They do not simply read the story.

They **discover** it.

They do not simply RSVP.

They **become part of the moment**.

### YUTEMU
**Temui kisah mereka.**
